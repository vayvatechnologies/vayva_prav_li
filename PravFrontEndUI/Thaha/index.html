Benefits of Yellow & White Butter

At Breezee Dairy Foods, we preserve the authentic goodness of traditional butter. Whether you prefer the rich taste of Yellow Butter or the freshness of White Butter, both are crafted using premium quality milk and time-honored methods.

🟡 Yellow Butter

Naturally rich, creamy and aromatic, Yellow Butter is prepared from premium cow cream, delivering exceptional taste and nutrition.

  • ✔ Naturally rich in Beta-Carotene (Vitamin A)
  • ✔ Supports healthy vision and immunity
  • ✔ Excellent source of healthy fats
  • ✔ Enhances the flavour of breads, rotis & curries
  • ✔ Perfect for baking and cooking
  • ✔ Smooth texture with rich golden colour
  • ✔ Made using traditional churning methods

⚪ White Butter

Freshly churned White Butter (Makhan) is soft, light and traditionally loved across Indian households for its natural taste.

  • ✔ Easy to digest compared to processed spreads
  • ✔ Naturally probiotic when freshly prepared
  • ✔ Helps support a healthy metabolism
  • ✔ Excellent source of natural energy
  • ✔ Free from artificial colours and flavours
  • ✔ Soft, creamy texture loved by all age groups
  • ✔ Ideal with parathas, chapatis and traditional dishes
